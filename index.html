Install

Install in four steps

No account, no store. Just a file.

Download

Tap Download for Android. Your browser may ask to keep the file; choose Keep.

Open the file

Open Phone.apk from the download notification or from Files › Downloads.

Allow the install

If asked, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser or file manager, then tap Install.

Finish setup

Open Phone › Setup Checklist and tap Fix on each item, so timed calls arrive on time.

On vivo or OriginOS

Also turn on Autostart, allow high background power use, and lock Phone in Recents. The OriginOS help screen inside the app shows each step.

Good to know

Questions, answered

What can it access?
NotificationsShows the incoming call. Full-screen callOpens the call over the lock screen. Alarms & remindersStarts timed calls at the exact time. Foreground serviceKeeps the ringtone playing. VibrationVibrates while ringing. Start at bootRestores your schedules after a restart. Battery optimisationStops the system delaying timed calls.

Not requested: contacts, call log, phone state, SMS, location, microphone, camera or internet.

Is Phone free?

Yes. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Why does my browser or phone show a warning?

Android warns about every app installed from outside Google Play, because it cannot vouch for the source. That is normal for direct downloads. Only install files from sources you trust, and compare the checksum below if one is shown.

Does it place or answer real calls?

No. It only draws a call screen inside its own app. It never touches your dialer, contacts or call log, and it cannot make or receive real calls.

Will it ring when the phone is locked?

Yes, once the Setup Checklist inside the app shows everything as Ready. On vivo and OriginOS, also follow the OriginOS help screen so the system does not pause the app.

Why isn’t it on Google Play?

Publishing on Google Play needs a paid developer account. For now Phone is shared directly as an APK file.

How do I update or remove it?

To update, download the newest file and install it over the old one. To remove it, go to Settings › Apps › Phone › Uninstall.

Use it responsibly

Phone is meant for your own phone: stepping out of a conversation, rehearsing, demos. Please don’t use it to imitate real people, banks or authorities, or to frighten or mislead anyone.
